# HTML Arcade privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

HTML Arcade is a personal, open-source iPhone and iPad player for HTML games, maintained by somethingwentwell. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising SDKs, or developer-operated backend. The developer does not collect your imported games, saves, browsing activity, or personal information through the app.

## What stays in the app

When you import a game, HTML Arcade copies its HTML file into app storage. The library stores its name, size, import date, most recent play date, and the source URL for games imported from the web. Each game has a separate persistent WebKit data store, which may contain that game's saves, localStorage, IndexedDB, cookies, and website data.

The Find browser uses WebKit's browser storage for the websites you visit. Imported files and app data may be included in an operating-system backup according to your device and iCloud settings. HTML Arcade does not operate a cloud sync or backup service.

## Internet connections and third-party games

Local, self-contained games can run without an internet connection. Games with remote assets, online accounts, multiplayer, ads, or other network features may connect to their own services.

Find sends searches to DuckDuckGo or opens the address you enter. Website visits, game downloads, and links to support or this privacy policy contact the relevant website directly. Those services can receive normal web-request information, such as your IP address, search query, requested URL, and cookies. Their own privacy policies apply. HTML Arcade does not receive a copy of that information on a developer-operated server.

HTML Arcade does not add tracking code to imported games and does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or games you choose to open. Imported content runs in WebKit without a native JavaScript bridge. Access to local game files is limited to that game's imported directory.

## Your controls

- Import only games and websites you choose to use.
- Play a self-contained game with Wi-Fi and cellular data off to prevent internet access during that session.
- Remove a game from the library to remove the imported copy and its separate WebKit data store. The original file in Files remains in place.
- Delete the app to remove its on-device app container. Operating-system backups are managed through your device settings.

## Support and policy updates

If you open a GitHub issue for support, GitHub receives the information you submit under its own privacy policy, and the issue may be public. Do not post passwords, tokens, personal save data, or other private information.

Questions or corrections can be submitted through [the project issue tracker](https://github.com/somethingwentwell/html-game-player/issues). Updates to this policy will be published here with a new date.
